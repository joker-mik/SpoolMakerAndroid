Spool Maker is a free Android app for reading and writing compatible NTAG216
NFC spool tags.

It can read material and weight information, write new tags, and import
Cura/UltiMaker material files. A local material library stores manufacturer,
material, color, GUID, and spool weight.

Spool Maker is community software and is not an official product of UltiMaker
or DA-Osborne. The source code is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
